Historic Tournament
First-ever 48-team World Cup opens in 2 days
104 matches, 39 days, three countries. Mexico vs South Africa kicks it off June 11 at Estadio Azteca. The final is July 19 at MetLife, NJ.

Title Race
Spain (+450) & France (+480) co-favorites; England (+700) close behind

Narrative
Key Storylines
⭐

Pulisic's Defining Moment

Christian Pulisic (AC Milan) is at the peak of his career, though he hasn't scored in 21 straight games. As the key attacker on American soil, this World Cup will define his legacy — for better or worse.

🎓

Pochettino's System

The 3-4-3/3-2-5 hybrid has looked dangerous when it clicks. Mixed warmup results — blown out by Belgium, beat Senegal, competitive vs Germany. Can the system hold under real tournament pressure?

👨‍👦

Soccer Dynasties: Reyna & Berhalter

Gio Reyna follows father Claudio (1994–2006) onto the WC roster. Sebastian Berhalter follows father Gregg (coach 2002–2022). Two father-son legacies — a genuine first in US soccer history.

🎯

Striker Trio: 56 Goals Combined

Balogun (Monaco, 19g), Pepi (PSV, 19g), Haji Wright (Coventry, 18g) combined for 56 goals this club season. For a team historically starved for strikers, this is a revolution. Who starts?

🏠

Home Crowd = 12th Man

SoFi at capacity for the opener, soccer-mad Seattle for game 2, then back to LA. Every previous strong host made a run: France 1998, Germany 2006. The crowd will be electric.

❌

Notable Absences

Diego Luna, Tanner Tessmann, Johnny Cardoso, and Patrick Agyemang all missed the final squad due to injury. Midfield depth is thinner than Pochettino would like going in.

Why This One's Different
What Makes 2026 Special

🌍 Biggest Ever

48 teams, 104 matches, 16 stadiums, three countries. The first-ever 48-team men's World Cup — 16 more teams than Qatar 2022. More nations, more underdogs, more chaos.

🏟️ Three Hosts

First WC ever co-hosted by three nations. Three separate opening ceremonies. Azteca hosts its third WC opening match (1970, 1986, 2026) — a genuine historical milestone.

👑 GOAT Final Chapter

Messi (Argentina) and Ronaldo (Portugal, age 41) making their 6th — almost certainly final — World Cup appearances. Two legendary careers reach their last chapter this summer.

🔀 New Bracket Format

Top 2 + 8 best 3rd-place teams advance. 32-team knockout bracket. Teams can afford one loss and potentially still advance — changes strategy in every group's final matchday.

🎉 American Soccer's Moment

MLS investment, a European-based generation in their prime, Pochettino at the helm. Playing at home. Unprecedented striker depth. This is what it's been building toward since 1994.

📍 Match #1,000

Tunisia vs Japan on June 21 will be the 1,000th match in FIFA World Cup history. Inside a tournament full of firsts, that milestone is itself one for the record books.
